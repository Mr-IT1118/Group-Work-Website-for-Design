THE FUNDAMENTALS

THREE SKILLS.
ENDLESS PRACTICE.

Choose a skill to explore its key cues and a simple practice idea.

01

BALL HANDLING

Stay in control.

A comfortable dribble gives you room to see the court and decide what comes next.

Keep these cues in mind

  • Use your fingertips to guide the ball.
  • Keep your knees comfortably bent and your body balanced.
  • Practice with both hands and gradually look up more.
Try it on court

Dribble in place with one hand, then the other. Once comfortable, walk slowly while keeping the ball under control.

02

PASSING

Move it with purpose.

A clear pass helps your teammate receive the ball ready for their next move.

Keep these cues in mind

  • Look at your teammate and choose a clear passing lane.
  • Step toward your target and follow through.
  • Show your hands when you are ready to receive a pass.
Try it on court

With a partner, alternate controlled chest passes and bounce passes. Start close enough that each pass can be caught comfortably.

03

SHOOTING

Find your rhythm.

Start close to the basket and build a repeatable motion before moving farther away.

Keep these cues in mind

  • Start from a balanced stance and look at the basket.
  • Use your shooting hand to guide the shot; let your other hand support the ball.
  • Extend smoothly and finish with a relaxed follow-through.
Try it on court

Take a few comfortable shots close to the hoop. Notice your balance and follow-through rather than rushing to shoot from farther away.

BETTER TOGETHER

THE SMALL THINGS
MAKE THE GAME.

Communicate.

Call for a pass, listen to teammates, and make your intentions clear.

Keep moving.

When you do not have the ball, look for space and ways to support the play.

Reset and learn.

A missed shot is one moment. Notice what happened and focus on the next play.
