THE IDEA

BUILT AROUND
BELONGING.

Court Phantoms is a student-created basketball brand concept with one simple idea: the game should feel open to everyone.

For the first-time player, that might mean learning a confident first dribble. For a lifelong fan, it might be the atmosphere of a close game. For friends, it could be one more pickup run before heading home.

We bring those experiences together through clear introductions to the game, a spirit of teamwork, and an appreciation for basketball culture.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

SHOW UP.
BRING YOURSELF.

These are the ideas behind our name, our design, and the way we talk about basketball.

01

Keep learning.

Being a beginner is part of the game. Ask questions, practice one skill at a time, and notice your progress.

02

Play together.

A good pass can matter as much as a good shot. Share the ball, communicate, and include your teammates.

03

Respect the court.

Respect the space, the people, and the game. Compete with effort and leave the court welcoming for the next group.
